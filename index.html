Questions

What shop owners ask us

What is Ledgro?

Ledgro is a mobile web app for kirana and small retail shops in India. It replaces the paper khata book with a digital credit book, sends WhatsApp payment reminders with the shop's UPI ID, makes GST bills with the shop's logo, and gives an end-of-day sales report. It is made by VJ Hokhmah Pvt. Ltd., with offices in Coimbatore (Tamil Nadu) and Ernakulam (Kerala).

How much does Ledgro cost?

Ledgro has three plans: Starter at ₹199 per month (₹1,990 per year), Growth at ₹399 per month (₹3,990 per year) and Pro at ₹499 per month (₹4,990 per year). Every plan starts with a 30-day free trial. Payments are made through Razorpay by UPI AutoPay, card or net banking.

Can Ledgro make GST bills?

Yes. Ledgro makes a Tax Invoice for regular GST shops, a Bill of Supply for composition scheme shops, and a simple bill for shops without GST. It calculates CGST and SGST, or IGST for other states, uses HSN codes, restarts invoice numbers every financial year and can add the shop's logo, Google Maps location and Instagram handle to the bill.

Which languages does Ledgro support?

Ledgro works in English, Tamil and Malayalam. Each customer can receive WhatsApp reminders in their own language. Hindi, Kannada and Telugu are planned next.

Other khata apps are free. Why pay for Ledgro?

Ledgro is built to bring your money back: reminders in your customer's language, branded GST bills, and a team that answers you on WhatsApp in Tamil or Malayalam. Most shops recover far more than ₹199 a month.

I don't have GST. Can I still use it?

Yes. Tell Ledgro you have no GST and your bills become simple receipts. If you're on the composition scheme, bills become bills of supply automatically.

Is my data safe?

Your khata and bills are encrypted with AES-256 on your own phone, locked by your 6-digit PIN. Ledgro's servers never receive them. Back up to your own Google Drive or email in one tap, and restore on a new phone any time.

I'm not good with phones.

Our team sets up your products, customers and bill design on the first visit. Most entries take three taps.
